SAMPLE DOCUMENT (fictional): Aurora Labs Employee Handbook, selected sections

About this sample
Aurora Labs is a fictional company invented for this demo. Nothing here describes a real employer.

Time off and leave
Aurora Labs offers 24 days of paid time off per year, accrued at 2 days per month. Unused days, up to a maximum of 6, carry over into January and expire on March 31. Sick leave is separate: 12 days per year, no carry-over. Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver, available after 9 months of employment. Public holidays follow the Karnataka state calendar, 11 days in 2026.

To request leave, employees submit a request in the HR portal at least 5 working days before the start date for leave longer than 3 days. Shorter absences need only manager approval over chat. Emergency leave is granted verbally and regularized in the portal within 2 working days of return.

Remote work policy
The default arrangement is hybrid: teams anchor in the Bengaluru office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Fully remote roles exist but are marked as such in the offer letter. Remote employees within India receive a one-time home office allowance of 18,000 rupees and a monthly internet reimbursement of 1,200 rupees. Co-working passes are available for employees outside Bengaluru through the facilities team, capped at 9,000 rupees per month.

Equipment remains company property. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. A lost device must be reported within 24 hours so it can be wiped remotely; the employee is not charged for a first incident.

Engineering practices
Code review is mandatory. Every pull request needs one approval from a teammate and passes continuous integration before merge. The main branch deploys automatically to staging; production deploys happen on Tuesdays and Thursdays after the 11 AM standup. Incident reviews are blameless and produce a written note within 5 working days.

Engineers get a learning budget of 25,000 rupees per year for courses, books, and conferences. The budget does not carry over. Talks at meetups are encouraged and count as work time when approved by a manager.

Expenses
Travel for work is booked through the operations team. Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours. Meal allowance during travel is 1,500 rupees per day within India. Expense reports are submitted within 30 days and reimbursed within 10 working days of approval.
